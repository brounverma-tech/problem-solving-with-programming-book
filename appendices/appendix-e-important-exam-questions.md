## E.7 Suggested Exam Preparation Strategy

- **First revision:** Definitions, full forms aur one-line concepts.
- **Second revision:** Difference questions aur diagrams.
- **Third revision:** Long answers ko headings aur points me prepare karein.
- **Daily practice:** Minimum three C++ programs bina dekhe likhein.
- **Final revision:** Syntax, common errors, outputs aur important numerical conversions check karein.

> Program answers me algorithm, code aur sample output dene se answer complete aur scoring banta hai.
